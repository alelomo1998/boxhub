App entry & shells

Today rxed is: log in → pick a box → one of three shells, all of which assume a box. An account with no gym has no shell at all — and that is the state every account starts in, because registration never creates a membership. These are the screens that give "no gym" and "several gyms" a place to live, drawn at both widths before any of them is built.

06

What the drawing settled

Four questions the sketches surfaced. Two were the user's call and were answered; two carry a recorded judgement, so a later critique argues with a reason rather than a blank.

Status copy — decided

Neutral for members, specific for admins. The label keys off the role you hold at that gym, not the status alone: an ADMIN at a PENDING gym is told "In review"; a member of a SUSPENDED gym is told only "Unavailable". A member cannot act on "suspended", and it is a fact about the owner's account rather than theirs.

Role labels — decided

Athlete / Coach / Admin. "Owner" was warmer but wrong for a gym whose admin is a hired manager; "Admin" is true whoever holds the role. The raw enum never reaches a screen, and all three strings are i18n-marked — today's picker prints BOX_ADMIN unmarked, which is the defect this replaces.

The volt budget on the hub's empty state — judged, re-openable

The law gives a plumbing screen one volt element; the empty state appears to have two, the active dock icon and the primary button. Taken: the dock's active icon is shell chrome, not screen content, and does not spend the screen's budget. The alternative reading says every shipped screen in the product is already over budget, which is the weaker reading. If the critique disagrees, the fix is one line — the empty-state button goes ghost.

Does landing on the hub clear your active gym? — judged, re-openable

Taken: no. Visiting /gyms leaves bh_active_box intact, so / still resumes and the hub can mark which gym you are in. The consequence is deliberate: "no gym" becomes a state you can look at without being a state you have entered — which is exactly what makes the Current chip mean anything.

Drawn with rxed's own tokens, faces and component styles — _tokens.scss, shell-header.component.ts, dock.component.ts, box-picker.page.ts. Dark-only because the product is dark-only. Nothing here is built yet; the spec follows once these are settled.
